Flagship product

Digital bank transfers for companies.

The main NorthBricks product takes payments from import to send: domestic and SEPA transfers, roles, status tracking, and exports for accounting.

How bank transfers work
  • Import payments Bring invoices or payout files into a single batch.
  • Approve with roles Dual control before money moves.
  • Send and track Follow each transfer from queued to completed.
