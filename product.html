Flagship product

Digital bank transfers for companies.

Too many teams still email invoices around and copy account numbers into internet banking. NorthBricks keeps the whole payment run in one place — this page describes the product, not a live banking connection.

How it works

Import, approve, send, track.

Import

Bring supplier invoices or a payout file into a payment batch.

Approve

The right people review amounts, accounts, and references before anything is sent.

Send

Release domestic and SEPA transfers as one controlled run.

Track

Follow status, then export what accounting needs for the books.

Features

Control before the money leaves.

Built for finance teams that need dual control, a readable history, and files that accounting can use.

  • SEPA and domestic transfers Pay suppliers in Sweden and across Europe from the same batch.
  • Roles and approvals Separate who prepares a payment from who signs it off.
  • Payment status See queued, sent, and completed without checking five inboxes.
  • Export for accounting Hand a clean file to your bookkeeping tools after the run.

Who it is for

Companies that pay many counterparties, often.

Agencies, wholesalers, and growing teams that have outgrown one person logging into the bank once a week.
